# CORPORATE CODE OF PROFESSIONAL CONDUCT

**Entity:** Harris Group of Hotels & Lodges (Pvt) Ltd.  
**Applicability:** All Technical Staff, IT Personnel, and Engineering Contractors  

---

### 1. PROFESSIONAL EXCELLENCE & INTEGRITY
Members of the technical team representing Harris Lodges are expected to uphold the highest standards of professional integrity, client data confidentiality, and respectful communication.

### 2. COMMITMENT TO GUEST TRUST
All engineering decisions must prioritize guest privacy, secure booking workflows, platform availability, and brand prestige. Code that compromises guest data security or introduces unauthorized backdoors is grounds for immediate termination and legal action.

### 3. ACCOUNTABILITY & TRANSPARENCY
Engineers and contributors must document architectural changes thoroughly, maintain clear audit trails, and report system anomalies promptly to engineering leadership.
