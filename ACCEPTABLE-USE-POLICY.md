# ACCEPTABLE USE & SYSTEM INTEGRITY POLICY

**System:** Harris Lodges Digital Infrastructure & Online Booking  
**Governing Body:** Harris Group of Hotels & Lodges (Pvt) Ltd.  

---

### 1. PERMITTED USE
The website, booking forms, WhatsApp link triggers, and email submission interfaces are provided solely for legitimate guests and corporate liaisons to enquire about, reserve, and manage hotel and conference bookings.

### 2. PROHIBITED ACTIVITIES
Users and automated clients are explicitly prohibited from:
1. Conducting Denial of Service (DoS/DDoS) attacks, automated scraping, vulnerability probing, or penetration testing without written authorization.
2. Submitting fraudulent, automated spam, or malicious booking requests designed to lock room inventory.
3. Injecting malicious code, SQL injection payloads, XSS scripts, or unauthorized tracking telemetry.
4. Using the platform to transmit unsolicited promotional materials or impersonate hotel management staff.

Violations of this policy will result in immediate IP blocking, referral to legal authorities, and potential civil prosecution.
