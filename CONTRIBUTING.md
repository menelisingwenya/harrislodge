# INTERNAL CONTRIBUTOR & WORK-FOR-HIRE IP ASSIGNMENT AGREEMENT

**Classification:** Internal Business Governance  
**Organization:** Harris Group of Hotels & Lodges (Pvt) Ltd.  

---

### 1. CLOSED REPOSITORY POLICY
**This repository does NOT accept public contributions, pull requests, or external open-source patches.**

Only pre-approved, contractually bound software engineers, designers, and authorized IT contractors employed by or engaged with Harris Group of Hotels & Lodges may commit code or modify repositories.

### 2. WORK-FOR-HIRE INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY ASSIGNMENT
All code, documentation, scripts, bug fixes, features, and assets submitted or pushed to this repository by authorized developers are created under a strict **Work-for-Hire** contractual arrangement. 

Upon submission, all worldwide rights, title, interest, patents, copyrights, and intellectual property rights in and to any contribution automatically vest exclusively and irrevocably with **Harris Group of Hotels & Lodges (Pvt) Ltd.**, with no retained royalties or personal claim by the contributor.

### 3. CODING STANDARDS & PROCEDURES
- All code must pass `npm run typecheck` and `npm run lint` before merging.
- No third-party packages with viral copyleft licenses (GPLv3, AGPL) may be added without legal approval.
- Commit messages must be written in professional, descriptive English detailing changes without informal acronyms or prefixes.
