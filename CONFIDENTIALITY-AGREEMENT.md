1. Maintain strict confidentiality regarding all code and operational mechanisms.
2. Never expose, fork, mirror, publish, or leak any part of this repository to public repositories, personal accounts, paste sites, AI model training platforms without consent, or third parties.

4. Notify the Company's Chief Technology Officer or Information Security Officer immediately upon becoming aware of any credential compromise or unauthorized access.
