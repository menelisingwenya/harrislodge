# PRIVACY & DATA PROTECTION POLICY

**Effective Date:** October 2026  
**Governing Body:** Harris Group of Hotels & Lodges (Pvt) Ltd.  

---

### 1. INFORMATION COLLECTED
During direct reservation workflows and site visits, we may collect:
- Personal identification data (Full Name, National ID / Passport where required for check-in)
- Contact information (Telephone/WhatsApp numbers, Email addresses)
- Stay parameters (Arrival dates, departure dates, room preferences, special accessibility or dietary requests)
- Technical data (IP addresses, browser user-agents, and session logs for fraud prevention)

### 2. USE OF INFORMATION
Guest information is exclusively processed to:
- Confirm, manage, and service room and conference bookings
- Communicate direct WhatsApp or Email booking itineraries
- Ensure physical property security and regulatory guest registry compliance
- We **never** sell, rent, or trade guest personal information to external marketing brokers.

### 3. DATA SECURITY & RETENTION
All reservation data transmitted via the website is encrypted using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3). Personal data is retained solely for the statutory period mandated by Zimbabwean hospitality regulations and accounting standards.
