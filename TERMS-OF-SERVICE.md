# TERMS OF SERVICE & GUEST RESERVATION AGREEMENT

**Last Updated:** October 2026  
**Operator:** Harris Group of Hotels & Lodges (Pvt) Ltd.  

---

### 1. RESERVATION GOVERNANCE
1. **Direct Booking:** Reservations submitted via our web application, WhatsApp Concierge, or official email constitute preliminary requests subject to branch availability confirmation.
2. **Pricing & Currency:** Room rates are quoted in United States Dollars (USD). Nightly rates exclude damages, additional meals, or unlisted premium amenities unless explicitly specified.
3. **Check-In & Check-Out Times:** Standard check-in is from 14:00 (2:00 PM). Check-out is by 11:00 (11:00 AM). Late check-outs must be pre-arranged with the front desk.

### 2. CANCELLATION & MODIFICATION POLICIES
1. Free cancellations are honored when communicated via WhatsApp or email at least 24 hours prior to the scheduled check-in date.
2. Failure to arrive without notice ("No-Show") may result in cancellation of subsequent reserved nights.

### 3. GUEST CONDUCT & PROPERTY SAFETY
Guests must adhere to branch policies regarding noise, safety, and occupancy limits. Harris Lodges reserves the right to decline service or remove individuals whose conduct threatens property security, staff welfare, or other guests' comfort.
